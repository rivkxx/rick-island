OPERATOR NOTES / 001

I’m a person with too many open tabs and a suspicious number of ongoing interests.

This is the space where those interests can exist next to each other without having to become a personal brand. I like learning how things work, making things, taking things apart, and following a question far beyond the point where it was probably sensible.

Some days that means law or politics. Other days it means music, code, games, writing, or an idea that started as a note at an inconvenient hour.
